# Visualization Concepts

## The Two-Panel Poster

### Structure
- **Left Panel:** Pre-AI GovCon state
- **Center Spine:** 12-gene genome wheel
- **Right Panel:** AI-era GovCon state

### Center Spine: The Genome Wheel
A circular diagram with three wedges:
- **Product wedge** (genes A1-A4)
- **Education wedge** (genes B1-B4)
- **Systems wedge** (genes C1-C4)

Each gene shown as a segment that can grow/shrink based on demand.

---

## Left Panel: Pre-AI State

### Supply/Demand Charts
Show traditional GovCon dynamics:
- High demand for: code output, syntax expertise, compliance artifacts
- Balanced: project management, documentation
- Low demand for: observability, cognitive enablement, governance automation

### Visual Elements
- Thick, slow-moving demand lines
- Large "code production" nodes
- Small/dim enablement nodes
- Prestige indicators attached to service delivery

---

## Right Panel: AI-Era State

### Oversupply Indicators
- Code output (flooding)
- Variants/rewrites
- Ticket completion velocity

### Critical Shortage Indicators
- Executable specifications
- Verification capabilities
- Telemetry/observability
- Governance automation
- Cognitive enablement
- Decision-making capacity

### Visual Elements
- Fast, branching demand lines
- Shrinking code production nodes
- Growing/bright enablement nodes
- Prestige indicators migrating to assurance/evidence

---

## The Loki Timeline Visualization

Inspired by the TV series, showing parallel timelines:

### Two Colors
- **Old world color** (e.g., amber/gold)
- **New world color** (e.g., blue/cyan)

### Non-Linear Paths
The path through the genome is not linear because of the product/service echo:
- Lines and demand passing through an organism
- The organism in the middle has a slider (product ↔ service)
- Genes get bigger/stronger based on inclination and capabilities

### Second Demand Disruptor
The human evolution dimension:
- Demand for humans to evolve into Mission vs Enablement
- Creates demand pressure on product/service capability genomes

---

## The Voltage/Impedance Visualization

Think of it as an electrical system:

### Demand Lines (External Field)
Three voltage sources:
1. Government Core Needs
2. Prestige/Inertia
3. AI Shock

### The Organism (Company)
- 12 genes as "org muscles"
- Product/service slider affecting impedance

### Impedance Factors
What slows current:
- Procurement rigidity
- Skill gaps
- Trust deficit
- Legacy architecture
- Risk aversion

### Key Nodes
- Permission (voltage level)
- Risk (inverse relationship with outcomes)
- Outcomes (mission delivery)
- Change Throughput (velocity)

### Flow Dynamics
- Genes act as conductance multipliers
- Higher gene expression = lower impedance for that capability
- AI shock increases available voltage but requires new paths
- Old high-impedance paths become bottlenecks

> "The lines of demand pass through the organizational genome differently based on whether you're building products or delivering services."
