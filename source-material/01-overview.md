# AI Personality Shift - Overview

## Summary

This is a conversation about how AI is changing software development and government contracting (GovCon). It develops a "12-gene genome" framework for understanding organizational capabilities and how AI disrupts the traditional model.

## Key Themes

1. The personality/skill shift required in AI-era software development
2. The collapse of traditional role boundaries (scrum master, product, developer)
3. Two new engineer "species": Mission Engineers and Enablement Engineers
4. The 12-gene genome framework for organizational capabilities
5. Application to Government Contracting (GovCon)
6. Electrical circuit metaphor for supply/demand modeling
7. Agent-based simulation for predicting market demand

## The Core Insight

> "AI didn't just 'make coding easier.' It moved engineering upward."

When syntax stops being the scarce skill, the pressure migrates into specification, intent, truth, and feedback. The object of work becomes: **executable intent + evidence**.
