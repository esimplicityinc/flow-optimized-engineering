# The AI Personality Shift

## The Problem

For ~40 years, software rewarded a very specific combo:

- **Syntax tolerance** (patience for rigid rules, delayed feedback, compiler-as-judge)
- **Solo throughput** (deep work, minimal coordination, "leave me alone and I'll ship")
- **Precision > persuasion** (being right mattered more than getting aligned)
- **Optimization mindset** (elegance, reduction, correctness)

We've been self-selecting for decades the opposite of what we need now.

## The Three Historical Tiers

- Coders
- Software Developers
- Engineers

## Then vs Now

### Then (syntax era): you won by being a high-trust compiler

The best people could:

- hold large systems in their head
- be careful and consistent
- grind through ambiguity until it became implementation
- get dopamine from correctness and closure

### Now (AI era): you win by being a fast decider with good taste

The best people can:

- form hypotheses quickly
- choose tradeoffs explicitly
- communicate intent to both machines and humans
- tolerate ambiguity while driving toward outcomes

## The New Selection Criteria

The shift is from **correctness execution** to **judgment velocity + system alignment**.

New traits that start mattering:

- **"Design sense" across stacks** (not just UI): what should exist and why
- **Rapid prototyping of intent** (sketches, specs, prompts)
- **Comfortable with "wrong-but-useful" early** (divergent thinking)
- **Stakeholder fluency** (can negotiate scope, tradeoffs, definitions of done)
- **Feedback loop obsession** (telemetry, adoption, outcomes)

## The Talent Pool Problem

Creative, bold, innovative personalities with soft skills - do we even have those people in software development? We've been self-selecting against them for decades.

## Natural Analogies

Similar "supply shock" scenarios from nature and technology:

1. **Printing press** → "writing" stops being scarce → new scarcity becomes editing, credibility, distribution
2. **Photography goes digital** → film expertise collapses → new scarcity becomes curation, editing, storytelling
3. **Predator release in nature** → vigilance species collapses → other traits dominate
4. **Nutrient floods in ecosystems** → specialist advantage collapses → selection pressure moves up-stack

## Genes That Die, Retreat, or Mutate

### Genes that likely die off:

- "Backlog priesthood" - ticket translation as core skill
- "Role purity + documentation-as-artifact"
- "Syntax heroism" - framework wizardry as identity

### Genes that survive by mutating:

- "Speed" mutates into "speed with receipts"
- "Documentation" mutates into "cognitive infrastructure"
- "Architecture" mutates into "constraint design"
- "Product management" mutates into "hypothesis management"
