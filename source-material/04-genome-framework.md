# The 12-Gene Genome Framework

## The Concept

Think of organizational capabilities as a **shared talent genome** with 12 "genes." Mission and Enablement orientations are different **expression profiles** (different weightings), not different species.

This is a selection set you can hire, coach, and promote against.

## The Structure: 3 Domains × 4 Layers = 12 Genes

---

## Domain A: Product (What We Decide)

### A1. Discovery

**Question:** Are we solving the right problem?

**Capability:** Can find the real problem (not the requested solution)

### A2. Tradeoffs

**Question:** What are we choosing and what are we giving up?

**Capability:** Makes crisp decisions under constraints

### A3. Adoption

**Question:** Will anyone actually use it?

**Capability:** Thinks in funnels (onboarding → habitual use)

### A4. Strategy

**Question:** Are we making the right bets in the right order?

**Capability:** Sequences decisions for maximum learning and impact

---

## Domain B: Education (How We Create Shared Understanding)

### B1. Mental Models

**Question:** Can we make the problem simple and shared?

**Capability:** Turns complexity into 2–3 simple rules

### B2. Communication

**Question:** Can we align humans fast across roles?

**Capability:** Bridges technical and business languages

### B3. Enablement

**Question:** Can we teach people to succeed by default?

**Capability:** Designs systems where the right path is obvious

### B4. Cognitive Ergonomics

**Question:** Can we reduce confusion and decision load?

**Capability:** Minimizes cognitive overhead in processes and tools

---

## Domain C: Systems (How We Make It Safe and Real)

### C1. Constraints

**Question:** What must always be true?

**Capability:** Defines and enforces guardrails

### C2. Verification

**Question:** How do we prove it's correct?

**Capability:** Tests, evaluations, evidence generation

### C3. Operability

**Question:** Can we run it, observe it, and recover?

**Capability:** Monitoring, observability, incident response

### C4. Governance

**Question:** Can we change safely over time?

**Capability:** Versioning, audit trails, deprecation management

---

## Expression Profiles

### Mission Engineer Profile

**High expression:** A1, A2, A3, B1, B2

**Moderate expression:** A4, B3, C1, C2

**Lower expression:** B4, C3, C4

### Enablement Engineer Profile

**High expression:** B3, B4, C1, C2, C3, C4

**Moderate expression:** A2, A3, B1, B2

**Lower expression:** A1, A4

---

## The CEO Pitch

> "Software delivery isn't one skill. It's a genome with 12 genes.  
> For 20 years, the environment rewarded a specific subset.  
> AI changes the environment—so the genes that matter change."

### The Key Line

> "AI creates an oversupply of code and a shortage of confidence."

---

## Supply/Demand Shifts (Pre-AI → Post-AI)

### Pre-AI Scarce (High Demand):

- Code output
- Syntax expertise
- Framework knowledge
- Implementation speed

### Post-AI Scarce (High Demand):

- Executable specifications (A1, A2)
- Verification capabilities (C2)
- Telemetry/observability (C3)
- Governance automation (C4)
- Cognitive enablement (B3, B4)
- Decision-making capacity (A2, A4)

### Post-AI Oversupply:

- Code output
- Variants/rewrites
- Ticket completion velocity
- Boilerplate generation
