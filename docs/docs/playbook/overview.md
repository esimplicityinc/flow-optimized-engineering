# The FOE Playbook

This is a field manual, not a philosophy book.

## What This Is

The FOE Playbook is **minimum viable process** - intentionally small and sharp. It contains the behaviors that matter when work is unclear, stakes are high, and trust is earned through action.

## Design Principles

This playbook is:

- **Normative, not aspirational**  -  describes how we actually behave, not how we wish we did
- **Concrete, not theoretical**  -  specific actions, not abstract frameworks
- **Behavior-focused, not tool-focused**  -  tools change; habits persist
- **Consumable in small pieces**  -  each page answers one question
- **Grounded in lived constraints**  -  acknowledges that people are busy, systems are messy, and perfection isn't the goal

## How It Maps to the Three Optimizations

| Optimization | What It Means | Playbook Pages |
|--------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Understanding** | Work is legible and traceable | How We Think About Speed, How Work Moves |
| **Confidence** | Risk is visible and bounded | How We Manage Risk, How We Decide |
| **Fast Feedback** | Learning happens before damage | How We Learn |

## Who This Is For

### Executives
You need to know that speed doesn't mean recklessness. These pages show you what to look for and what to ask.

### Government Partners
You need evidence that change is controlled. These pages explain our defaults in language that maps to your concerns.

### Engineers
You need clarity on expectations. These pages tell you what "good" looks like without burying you in process.
