# How We Manage Risk

<span class="badge badge--primary">Executives</span> <span class="badge badge--info">Government</span>

> **"Risk doesn't disappear when you slow down. It just hides longer."**

## Our Approach

1. **Detect risk early**  -  through small changes, fast feedback, and visible work
2. **Contain it locally**  -  failures are bounded, not cascading
3. **Make it explainable**  -  every risk decision has a rationale that can be audited

## What You'll See

- **Clear change narratives**  -  what changed, why, and what we expected to happen
- **Evidence tied to decisions**  -  not just outcomes, but the reasoning that led to them
- **Fewer "surprises at the end"**  -  problems surface when they're cheap to fix

## What This Looks Like

| Traditional Risk Management | FOE Risk Management |
|-----------------------------|---------------------|
| Big releases, big testing phases | Small changes, continuous validation |
| Risk registers updated quarterly | Risk signals visible daily |
| Post-mortems after incidents | Learning before damage |
| "We'll catch it in QA" | "We'll know within hours" |

## The Hidden Cost of Slowness

Slowing down feels safe. But slow cycles mean:

- Feedback arrives late
- Problems compound before detection
- Recovery requires heroics
- Risk accumulates invisibly

**Speed with visibility beats slowness with hope.**

## Maps To

**Confidence Optimization**  -  Stakeholders can trust what they see because risk is surfaced, not suppressed.
