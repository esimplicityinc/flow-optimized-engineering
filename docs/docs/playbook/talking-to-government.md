# How We Talk to Government Partners

<span class="badge badge--info">External</span>

## Our Default Posture

**Calm. Clear. Evidence-first.**

Government partners have seen vendors over-promise and under-deliver. They've been burned by hype. They've sat through presentations full of jargon and empty of substance.

We don't do that.

## What We Show

### How change is controlled

- Changes are small, traceable, and reversible
- Every deployment has a rollback plan
- Nothing goes to production without evidence it works

### How risk is detected

- Automated testing catches problems before humans see them
- Monitoring alerts us before users notice
- We measure what matters, not what's easy

### How learning happens continuously

- Incidents are analyzed and improvements implemented
- We get better every week, not every contract cycle
- Evidence of improvement is available on request

## What We Avoid

| We Avoid | We Prefer |
|----------|-----------|
| Jargon | Plain language |
| Hype | Evidence |
| Claims without receipts | Demonstrations |
| "Trust us" | "Here's how you can verify" |
| Promises | Proof |

## Translating FOE for Government Context

| FOE Concept | Government Translation |
|-------------|------------------------|
| Fast feedback | Continuous monitoring and early detection |
| Small changes | Incremental delivery, reduced risk per deployment |
| Psychological safety | Culture that surfaces problems early |
| Observable progress | Auditable change history with clear rationale |
| Reversible decisions | Rollback capability, bounded blast radius |

## Questions We're Ready to Answer

- How do you ensure changes don't break existing functionality?
- What happens when something goes wrong?
- How do you handle security vulnerabilities?
- Can you demonstrate compliance with [specific requirement]?
- What evidence do you have that this approach works?

## The Underlying Message

We're not asking you to trust us.

We're showing you the system that makes trust unnecessary - because the evidence speaks for itself.

## Maps To

**Trust through transparency** - adapted for audit culture, where "show your work" isn't optional.
