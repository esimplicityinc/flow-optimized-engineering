# Field Guides: The Simple Version

**Field Guides help teams work better by sharing what actually works.**

That's it. No complicated frameworks. No buzzwords.

## The Core Idea

Your teams try new approaches all the time. Some work great. Some don't. The problem? That knowledge usually stays trapped in people's heads or gets lost when projects end.

Field Guides fix this by:

1. **Collecting methods** - Practical approaches that teams have tried
2. **Tracking evidence** - Did it actually work? How do we know?
3. **Showing maturity** - Is this battle-tested or still observational?

## What You'll See

Each guide has a simple health indicator:

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Proven** | Multiple teams have validated this works |
| **Validated** | Evidence shows it works |
| **Observing** | We're testing it now |
| **Hypothesized** | Proposed but not yet tested |

The more proven methods in a guide, the more confident you can be in adopting it.

## Why This Matters

- **Reduce risk** - Know what's tested before rolling it out
- **Move faster** - Don't reinvent approaches that already exist
- **Build on success** - Learn from what's working across teams

## What You Don't Need to Worry About

- The technical implementation details
- How observations are structured
- The specifics of maturity calculations

Your teams handle all of that. You just need to know: *Is this ready for broader adoption?* The maturity indicator answers that question at a glance.

## Questions?

Talk to your team leads about which guides are relevant to your current initiatives and their maturity levels.
