# Humans in the Loop

If machines write the code, what do humans do?

Humans do what machines can’t—and what machines shouldn’t.

## Four human irreducibles

1. **Judgment** — tradeoffs without a clean math answer.
2. **Ethics** — what should happen, not just what can.
3. **Accountability** — someone must answer for outcomes.
4. **Intent** — deciding what success even means.

FOE isn’t about removing humans.

It’s about removing tedium so humans can stay responsible without becoming bottlenecks.

The cockpit doesn’t replace the pilot.

It makes the pilot effective at supersonic speed.
