# People Changes

Tools arrive in boxes. Processes arrive in PDFs. People arrive with identities.

That’s why this part is hard.

FOE asks people to trade one kind of value for another: from being indispensable to building systems that don’t require them.

That’s not just a skill shift.

That’s an identity shift.

## The skill evolutions

### Ops: guardians → stewards

Stop protecting production by presence. Protect it by automation, policy, and paved paths.

### Security: gatekeepers → guardrail builders

Stop being the department of “no.” Become the team that makes “yes” safe at scale.

### Developers: code writers → system owners

Own outcomes and operability. Write code you can debug at 2 AM.

### Leaders: output managers → flow optimizers

Stop managing activity. Start shaping environments. Measure flow, remove systemic blockers, and fund institution-building.

## The hero problem

Heroes are real.

And they’re a liability.

If your system needs heroics, your system is unsafe.

Celebrate the boring deployment.

Promote the engineer who removed the need for escalation.

## Incentives decide behavior

Reward automation over heroics, teaching over knowing, and system improvement over local productivity.

Or don’t.

And watch transformation die slowly in polite meetings.
