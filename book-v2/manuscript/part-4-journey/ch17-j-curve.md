# The J-Curve

Transformation has a shape. It’s not a straight line. It’s a J: things get worse, then they get better, then they get better than they were.

## Why the dip happens

New habits are slow before they are automatic. New tools are awkward before they are fluent. Parallel systems double cognitive load. Debt you used to ignore becomes visible. Identities get threatened.

If nothing dips, you’re probably relabeling.

Not transforming.

## How long

Longer than you want.

Shorter than doing nothing.

For real Phase 2 → Phase 3 change, think in **12–18 months** for clear compounding benefits.

## How to survive it

1. **Name the dip before it arrives.**
2. **Track leading indicators** while lagging indicators look ugly.
3. **Celebrate process milestones** (first boring deploy, first auto-rollback, first week with zero exceptions).
4. **Protect the transformation** from “just this once” exceptions.

## How transformations die

Leadership flinches, heroics “save the day,” victory gets declared early, and the transition gets underfunded.

Survive the dip.

That’s the assignment.
