# The Maturity Model

Phase is what you *have*. Maturity is how predictably you operate.

Predictability beats sophistication.

Every time.

## The five levels

0. **Fragile** — you find out late.
1. **Visible** — you can see what happened.
2. **Repeatable** — you can change safely.
3. **Flowing** — the system teaches you as you use it.
4. **Adaptive** — you absorb change without destabilizing.

## The rule

> **Maturity is outcomes, not tooling.**

Not “we have Kubernetes,” but whether deployments succeed predictably, problems surface before customers, three people can explain the same system the same way, and you can recover without heroics.

## Diagnose by the triangle

Assess maturity across the FOE principles: **understanding** (is knowledge in systems or heads?), **fast feedback** (how long until you learn you’re wrong?), and **confidence** (do teams act, or freeze?).

Don’t use this as a scorecard.

Use it as a mirror.
