# The Living Doctrine

FOE is not a ceremony set.

It’s a doctrine.

And doctrine only matters when pressure hits.

## The argument (compressed)

Speed and safety are not tradeoffs; they’re the same capability. Human cognition is the bottleneck. The doctrine is three principles—understanding, fast feedback, confidence—and adoption is sociotechnical: people, process, and technology move together. The journey has phases, a Phase 3 bottleneck, and a J-curve. AI makes the need urgent by removing the old margin for error.

## Why it’s “living”

Principles stay stable.

Practices evolve.

Tools change.

The doctrine should absorb new observations, retire bad methods, and strengthen what works.

## What to do now

Don’t start with a grand reorg.

Start with one move that increases capability: make one system more understandable, make one feedback loop faster, or make one deployment safer.

Build one institution.

Measure one outcome.

Then repeat.

Go faster by knowing more.
