# Building Institutions

If your delivery system depends on people remembering to do the right thing, it will fail.

Not because people are bad.

Because pressure is real.

Institutions are how you make discipline survive pressure—lightweight, but real.

## What an institution is

An institution is a mechanism that makes desired behavior the default. Not a memo. Not a value. Not “we should.” A mechanism.

## Anatomy (keep it tight)

1. **Purpose** — what behavior does it create?
2. **Mechanism** — how does it work (concretely)?
3. **Trigger** — when does it fire?
4. **Enforcement** — what happens on violation?
5. **Owner** — who is accountable?
6. **Measure** — how do we know it’s working?

If you can’t answer these, you don’t have an institution.

You have a wish.

## Core FOE institutions

1. **CI gate** — broken code can’t merge.
2. **Review standard** — review is for comprehension, not ceremony.
3. **Definition of done** — “done” includes evidence.
4. **Automated deploy** — the pipeline is the path.
5. **Learning loop** — incidents become guardrails.

## The design rule

Prefer **technical enforcement** over social enforcement.

Humans get tired.

Automation doesn’t.
