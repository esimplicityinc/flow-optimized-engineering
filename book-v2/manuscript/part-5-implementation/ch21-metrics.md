# Metrics That Matter

Metrics are your gauges.

Without them, you’re driving fast in fog.

With them, you can go fast with precision.

## Start with DORA

**Deployment frequency. Lead time. Change failure rate. MTTR.**

They’re hard to game as a set and they map directly to delivery capability.

## Measure flow, not activity

Don’t worship story points, tickets closed, or lines of code.

Those are outputs.

You want outcomes.

## Make metrics executable

The best metrics don’t just inform.

They trigger gates, rollbacks, and scaling.

If metrics only trigger meetings, you’re still operating at human speed.
