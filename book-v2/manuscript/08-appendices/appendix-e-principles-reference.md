# Appendix E: The Three Principles (One Page)

## Understanding

**Aim:** make systems legible.

**Build:** bounded scope, explicit interfaces, observable state, traceable causality, progressive disclosure.

## Fast feedback

**Aim:** shorten time-to-disconfirmation.

**Build:** layered tests, automated pipelines, production observability.

## Confidence

**Aim:** bound risk so action stays rational.

**Build:** guardrails, reproducible environments, quality by default, bounded rollouts, rollback.
