# Appendix D: Self-Assessment (Fast)

## Phase honesty checks

- Do you ever change prod outside the declared source of truth?
- Do exceptions exist as a standing category?
- Can any team deploy without a hero?
- Do metrics trigger automated action or meetings?

## Triangle diagnosis

**Understanding weak** if:
- onboarding is slow,
- surprises are common,
- “don’t touch that” zones exist.

**Feedback weak** if:
- CI is slow,
- users find issues first,
- failures recur.

**Confidence weak** if:
- decisions escalate,
- deploys are scary events,
- teams freeze on important changes.

## Readiness questions

- Do leaders fund the dip (time + money + air cover)?
- Is enforcement real (or negotiable)?
- Do you have a platform path teams actually adopt?
