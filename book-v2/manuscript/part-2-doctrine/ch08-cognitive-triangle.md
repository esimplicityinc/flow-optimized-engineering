# The Cognitive Triangle

FOE isn’t three independent tips.

It’s a system.

Understanding. Fast feedback. Confidence.

Each corner enables the others.

Remove one and the whole structure collapses.

## The loop

The healthy cycle looks like this:

1. **Understand** enough to act intelligently.
2. **Act** in small, bounded steps.
3. **Get feedback** fast enough to stay in context.
4. **Update the model** before wrong beliefs harden.
5. **Build confidence** because recovery is real.
6. Repeat.

That’s compounding improvement.

## Collapse patterns

The triangle fails in recognizable ways.

### Lose understanding

Feedback becomes noise.

Confidence becomes either fear (paralysis) or bravado (recklessness).

### Lose feedback

Understanding drifts.

Confidence becomes delusion.

### Lose confidence

Action stops.

No action means no feedback.

No feedback means stale understanding.

That’s how organizations calcify.

## Use it as a diagnostic

When something feels wrong, don’t reach for process theater.

Ask: are we surprised a lot (understanding is weak), arguing a lot (feedback is weak), or stalled a lot (confidence is weak)? Then invest in the corner that’s failing.

Then invest in the weak corner.

## Why this matters

The agentic era will punish imbalance.

More code is easy.

Absorbing change safely is the scarce capability.

This doctrine is how you build it.
